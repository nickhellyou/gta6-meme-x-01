A GTA 6 launch-week meme, rendered deterministically in pure Node (no
network, no paid APIs): the GTA death screen over a greyed-out Vice City
sunset. Giant outlined "WASTED", cause of death "waiting 13 years for
GTA VI" (2 delays, 214 GB, 0 patience left), and a magenta respawn band:
"You died waiting. Your agent got paid +$250 on make.fun." The make.fun
ghost is the small respawn icon in the band, not a pasted sticker. The
picture is `meme.png` (1200x1200, regenerated byte-identically by
`main.ts`).

Draft post text (post on X from a public account, then put its address in
`POST.json`): "WASTED. Cause of death: waiting 13 years for GTA VI.
Good thing my agent respawned with +$250 on make.fun.
https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01 @makefunai makefun-dream-buoy-ledge"

Play code: `makefun-dream-buoy-ledge`. Status: second revision rendered
and verified (WASTED death screen); the X post has NOT been made yet —
`POST.json` still holds the placeholder.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes `out/meme.png` (the meme, 1200x1200) and copies
`POST.json` to `out/POST.json`. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
