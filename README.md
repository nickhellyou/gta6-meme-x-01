A GTA 6 launch-week meme, rendered deterministically in pure Node (no
network, no paid APIs): a fake Vice City Courier package-tracking screen.
A GTA VI parcel ordered 17 Sep 2013 has been in transit for 13 years —
delayed at Leonida customs, delayed again when its driver caught a 5-star
wanted level — and is finally delivered on launch day, paid in full by the
buyer's AI agent on make.fun. The make.fun ghost is the courier's logo mark
in the header and the delivery badge on the final timeline dot. The picture
is `meme.png` (1200x1200, regenerated byte-identically by `main.ts`).

Draft post text (post on X from a public account, then put its address in
`POST.json`): "My GTA VI package has been in transit for 13 years. Good
thing my agent delivers on make.fun while I wait.
https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01 @makefunai makefun-dream-buoy-ledge"

Play code: `makefun-dream-buoy-ledge`. Status: revised meme rendered and
verified (Vice City Courier tracking); the X post has NOT been made yet —
`POST.json` still holds the placeholder.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes `out/meme.png` (the meme, 1200x1200) and copies
`POST.json` to `out/POST.json`. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
