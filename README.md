network, no paid APIs): a monthly billing statement for X Premium. The
joke is 13 years of paying for a blue checkmark while waiting for GTA
VI — the meme is posted ON X and the bill is FOR X Premium. The table

PRIORITY REPLIES, 0 SEEN BY ROCKSTAR; EDIT BUTTON, USED 0 TIMES;
GROK: WHEN IS GTA VI OUT?, ASKED 4,811 TIMES; TIMELINE REFRESHES,
6,927,840; AD-FREE FEED, 214 ADS SEEN; LONG POSTS, 4,811 POSTED, 0
READ; BOOKMARK: GTA VI DATE (REAL!), SAVED 13 YEARS AGO — and the
total is $1,264.00, "THAT'S 15 COPIES OF GTA VI. I OWN ZERO.", with a
red PAID stamp (BY MY AI AGENT ON MAKE.FUN) and a magenta band at the
bottom as the document's own resolution: "Paid in full by my AI agent
with a bounty it earned on make.fun." The ghost is the billing seal in
the header and the mascot of the payoff band. The picture is `meme.png`
(1200x1200, regenerated byte-identically by `main.ts`).

`POST.json`): "13 years of X Premium cost me $1,264. 4,811 Grok asks,

makefun-dream-buoy-ledge"

Play code: `makefun-dream-buoy-ledge`. Status: sixth revision rendered
and verified (X Premium billing statement); the X post has NOT been
made yet — `POST.json` still holds the Rockstar post address.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes `out/meme.png` (the meme, 1200x1200) and copies
`POST.json` to `out/POST.json`. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
