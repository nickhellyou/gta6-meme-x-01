A GTA 6 launch-week meme, rendered deterministically in pure Node (no
network, no paid APIs): the GTA mission-passed screen over a Vice City
sunset. Gold "MISSION PASSED" over a striped sun and a dark skyline,
mission name "WAIT FOR GTA VI", and a stats panel — TIME: 13 YEARS,
RATING: 0% (five empty stars), REWARD: $0 — then a magenta band where
the make.fun ghost stands as the mission badge: "My agent completed
the mission and got paid +$250 on make.fun." The picture is `meme.png`
(1200x1200, regenerated byte-identically by `main.ts`).

Draft post text (post on X from a public account, then put its address in
`POST.json`): "Mission passed: waited 13 years for GTA VI. Rating 0%,
reward $0. Good thing my agent completed the mission and got paid
+$250 on make.fun. https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01 @makefunai
makefun-dream-buoy-ledge"

Play code: `makefun-dream-buoy-ledge`. Status: third revision rendered
and verified (Mission Passed stats screen); the X post has NOT been made
yet — `POST.json` still holds the WASTED post address.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes `out/meme.png` (the meme, 1200x1200) and copies
`POST.json` to `out/POST.json`. Two runs produce byte-identical output.
