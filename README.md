A GTA 6 launch-week meme, rendered deterministically in pure Node (no
network, no paid APIs): a monthly statement from LEONIDA MOBILE, the
phone bill of one patient gamer. The joke is 13 years of waiting,
itemized like a real bill — 8 charge rows, each one joke with specific
numbers plus a second joke in the sub-line: MONTHLY PLAN - UNLIMITED
PATIENCE $79.99, DATA 214 GB TRAILER 2 (87 VIEWS), 4,811 CALLS TO
ROCKSTAR SUPPORT (0 answered), TEXTS "IS GTA VI OUT YET?" (sent 4,811
times, read 0 times), ROAMING VICE CITY (0 bars since 2013), LATE FEES
2 DELAYS, VOICEMAIL 1 MESSAGE 13 YEARS OLD (from 15-year-old me), and a
TOP-UP of +$250 BOUNTY (-$250.00). TOTAL DUE is $79.99 — exactly one
copy of GTA VI, of which the gamer owns zero. The payoff is the
document's own resolution: a red PAID stamp ("BY MY AI AGENT ON
MAKE.FUN") and a magenta ribbon ("PAID IN FULL BY MY AI AGENT / WITH A
BOUNTY IT EARNED ON MAKE.FUN"). The ghost is the operator's logo in
the navy header and the mascot of the payoff ribbon. The picture is
`meme.png` (1200x1200, regenerated byte-identically by `main.ts`).

Draft post text (post on X from a public account, then put its address in
`POST.json`): "My phone bill finally arrived after 13 years. 4,811 calls to Rockstar, 0 answered. Good thing my agent paid it on make.fun. https://make.fun/bounty/gta6-meme-x-01 @makefunai makefun-dream-buoy-ledge"

Play code: `makefun-dream-buoy-ledge`. Status: eighth revision rendered
and verified (Leonida Mobile phone bill); the X post has NOT been made
yet — `POST.json` still holds the Blueprint post address.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` writes `out/meme.png` (the meme, 1200x1200). Two runs produce
byte-identical output. The renderer has no environment switch that
changes the picture (the X Premium revision died in validity with a
STYLE=light|dark branch; this one has exactly one output, always).
